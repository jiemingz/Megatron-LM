Complete forward/backward boundary calls including resets, gradient accumulation and final reductions; no GEMMs or optimizer. CUDA graph replay excludes Python/autograd and allocator CPU overhead.
Two independent serial processes on GPU0 under /tmp/nano_cooperative_gpu0.lock. Three pools, 60 calls/graph, five replays/sample, nine randomized rounds/process. Aggregate latency is median of all 18 samples.
No profiler was active. These are boundary kernel speedups, not end-to-end model speedups.
